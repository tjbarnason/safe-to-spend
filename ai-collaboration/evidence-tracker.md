# Evidence Tracker

Research sources informing product decisions, with confidence ratings and traceability.

## Rating Scale
- **High** — Large-scale quantitative research, authoritative source, directly relevant
- **Medium-High** — Authoritative source, somewhat indirect relevance or smaller sample
- **Medium** — Directionally useful, requires inference or combination with other sources
- **Low** — Anecdotal or limited applicability

## Evidence Standards Applied

### Facts (directly measured, high confidence)
- Income variability rates (SHED survey, n=11,000+)
- BNPL late payment rates (SHED survey)
- Emergency savings shortfall (SHED survey)
- Section 1033 regulatory status (CFPB published rule)

### Observations (measured indirectly, medium-high confidence)
- Digital banking satisfaction plateau (J.D. Power methodology, industry sample)
- Alert fatigue patterns (J.D. Power survey responses)
- Overdraft fee impact distribution (CFPB regulatory analysis)

### Inferences (derived from evidence, medium confidence)
- Decision uncertainty is the core unaddressed problem (synthesis of multiple sources)
- Community FIs are the optimal distribution channel (market structure + incentive analysis)
- Three confidence moments cover the member journey (behavioral analysis of decision timing)

## Gaps and Limitations

- No direct user research with target members (would strengthen in pilot phase)
- J.D. Power data is industry-aggregate; individual FI behavior may vary
- Gig economy size estimates vary by definition and methodology
- "Decision uncertainty" as a named problem is our framing — not an established category
