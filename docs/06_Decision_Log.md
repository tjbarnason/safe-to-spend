# Decision Log

All key product and technical decisions, with alternatives considered and rationale.

| # | Date | Decision | Alternatives Considered | Rationale | Confidence | Impact |
|---|------|----------|------------------------|-----------|------------|--------|
| 1 | 2025-01 | Problem-first discovery (start with evidence, not solution) | Solution-first (build "safe to spend" and work backward), Feature-first (list competitor features and match) | Evidence reveals the real problem is decision uncertainty, not balance visibility. Starting with the problem ensures the solution actually addresses consumer need. | High | Foundational — shaped entire product direction |
| 2 | 2025-01 | Behavioral segmentation (variable-income adults as primary) | Demographic segmentation (millennials, low-income), Product segmentation (checking account holders) | Income variability is the behavioral driver of decision uncertainty. Demographic cuts miss the causal mechanism. 29% of adults is a large, underserved segment. | High | Target audience, positioning, messaging |
| 3 | 2025-01 | Decision uncertainty thesis (not management, but uncertainty) | Financial literacy thesis, Overspending thesis, Budget adherence thesis | Management tools exist and have plateaued. Uncertainty is unaddressed. The moment of stress is the moment of decision, not the monthly review. | High | Core product thesis — everything derives from this |
| 4 | 2025-01 | B2B2C through community FIs | D2C app, Embedded in neobank, API-only for developers | FIs have the data and trust relationship. They need differentiation. D2C requires user acquisition spend and data aggregation. B2B2C leverages existing relationships. | High | Business model, distribution, technical architecture |
| 5 | 2025-01 | Safe to Spend evolution (not replacement) | Build from scratch with new framing, Copy existing implementations, Build budgeting tool instead | Existing consumer mental model of "safe to spend" is valid but incomplete. Evolving it preserves intuition while adding projection, simulation, and protection. | High | Product framing, competitive positioning |
| 6 | 2025-01 | Open banking infrastructure as "why now" | AI/ML maturity as why now, Gen-Z digital natives as why now, Pandemic behavioral shift as why now | CFPB 1033 creates the regulatory certainty and data access required. Without reliable data pipes, the product cannot function. Infrastructure is the true enabling condition. | High | Timing justification, technical feasibility |
| 7 | 2025-01 | Outcome over engagement (optimize for confidence, not sessions) | Engagement metrics (DAU, session length), Notification engagement, Feature adoption rates | Engagement optimization creates dark patterns. Our thesis is that the best outcome is a member who checks once and feels confident. Metrics should reflect member well-being. | High | Success metrics, design philosophy, incentive alignment |
| 8 | 2025-01 | Three confidence moments (ambient, active, protective) | Single "safe to spend" number, Dashboard-only with no simulation, Alerts-only with no dashboard | Different moments serve different needs. Ambient = daily confidence. Active = decision-time clarity. Protective = anticipatory safety. All three together create comprehensive coverage. | High | Product architecture, UX structure, feature scope |
| 9 | 2025-01 | Next.js 14+ with App Router, TypeScript | React SPA, Vue.js, Svelte, Rails/HTMX | Server Components enable fast data-heavy rendering. TypeScript provides type safety for financial calculations. Ecosystem maturity. shadcn/ui for accessible components. | High | Technical stack, developer experience, performance |
| 10 | 2025-01 | No database for MVP (in-memory mock data) | SQLite, PostgreSQL, JSON files | Simplifies deployment and demonstration. Domain logic is the focus, not persistence. MockDataProvider is deterministic, enabling reproducible testing. Database is a Phase 2 concern. | Medium-High | Deployment simplicity, testing, scope control |
| 11 | 2025-01 | Provider abstraction (DataProvider interface) | Direct data access, Hard-coded mock data, API-first with OpenAPI | Interface enables swapping MockDataProvider for real integrations (Plaid, MX, core banking) with zero domain logic changes. Future-proofs without over-engineering. | High | Extensibility, testability, separation of concerns |
| 12 | 2025-01 | Domain-first testing (property-based + unit) | UI testing first, Integration testing only, Manual QA | Financial calculations must be provably correct. Property-based tests validate universal invariants (arithmetic consistency, ordering, threshold correctness). Unit tests validate specific scenarios. | High | Quality assurance, correctness guarantee, confidence in refactoring |
| 13 | 2025-01 | Precision over recall (fewer predictions, higher confidence) | Show everything detected, Use ML confidence scores, Let members choose threshold | A false positive (predicting an obligation that doesn't exist) erodes trust more than a false negative (missing one). Members must trust the numbers to act on them. | High | Detection thresholds, confidence levels, member trust |
| 14 | 2025-01 | No red color in financial displays | Standard red/green for negative/positive, Red for alerts only, User-configurable colors | Red triggers anxiety and alarm. Our thesis is calm over urgent. Amber communicates "attention needed" without triggering financial stress. Green for comfortable. Neutral for informational. | Medium-High | Visual design, emotional tone, accessibility (color-blind users benefit from non-red/green dichotomy) |
| 15 | 2025-01 | Next Best Actions as core differentiator | Show insight only (no action), List all possible actions, Single "fix it" button | The strategic evolution is Reporting → Predicting → Recommending. Stopping at prediction leaves the member wondering "now what?" Connecting intelligence to specific, executable banking actions (Early Pay, Transfer, Protect) transforms Safe to Spend from an analytics feature into an orchestration layer. | High | Product differentiation, platform value, revenue enablement |
| 16 | 2025-01 | Embedded banking dashboard (not standalone tool) | Dedicated Safe to Spend app, Single-card widget, Full-screen takeover | Safe to Spend should feel like one premium capability within a banking experience — not the entire application. This demonstrates platform thinking: greeting, account balance, Safe to Spend, recommendations, and activity feed create a complete banking dashboard that a credit union could ship. | High | Product positioning, Nymbus relevance, reviewer perception |
| 17 | 2025-01 | Early Pay as demonstrable banking capability | Conceptual mockup only, Alert-based suggestion (no page), Savings transfer only | An interactive Early Pay flow ($50-$500 advance selection with impact preview) demonstrates that Safe to Spend intelligence connects to real banking products. This proves the orchestration thesis: intelligence identifies the need, recommendation surfaces the action, capability fulfills it. | Medium-High | Product completeness, platform demonstration, commercial thinking |
